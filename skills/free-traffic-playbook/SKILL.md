# Free Traffic Playbook ($0 Spend)

## Purpose
Get the same outcome as paid advertising — qualified traffic that converts — using only free levers. Every lever below is $0; each lists what the agent does vs. what needs the owner.

## The Levers (highest value first)

### 1. Google Merchant Center FREE listings — the free Google Ads
Free product listings on the Google Shopping tab. Same placement family as paid Shopping, zero spend.
- **Agent:** product feed already generated (116 products / 799 variants) at `~/workspace/your_files/wagb-feed` — validate it against Google's feed spec (required attributes: id, title, description, link, image_link, price, availability, brand, gtin/mpn) and fix rejections before upload.
- **Owner:** create the Merchant Center account, verify the domain, and upload/connect the feed — Google-account actions only he can do.
- Then monitor the Merchant Center diagnostics for disapprovals weekly.

### 2. Organic video pipelines (already running — protect the cadence)
Instagram Daily Five (07:37 ET), TikTok 5/day (10:37 ET), weekly 91-post Meta pipeline (Mondays 09:37 ET). Free reach is the store's #1 lever. Never let a pipeline silently fail — check scheduled tabs when a run reports an error.

### 3. SEO quick wins
Live theme has the SEO foundation. Recurring: product titles/descriptions with real search terms, internal links from blog/content to product pages, image alt text, Google Search Console (needs owner to connect) for query data.

### 4. Email capture + Klaviyo (already live)
Welcome Series and Abandoned Cart flows are LIVE. Free growth: on-site capture (the bundle/quiz widgets in `~/workspace/your_files/`), list growth rate, flow revenue per recipient.

### 5. Google Business Profile
Free brand presence in Google search/maps. Owner creates; agent supplies copy, photos, product posts.

### 6. AOV before paid
Paid can't scale profitably at $15.99 AOV (~$9.60 gross profit vs. $0.70–$1.00 CPCs). Free fix: bundles, cart upsells, and "frequently bought together" using the approved free apps — push AOV toward $30+ so a future paid test can break even.

## Output Contract
When asked for "free alternatives," lead with lever 1 (Merchant Center free listings) — it is the direct free substitute for Google Ads — then 2–4. Always split agent-actions vs. owner-actions; never claim a lever is live until its owner-side step is done.

## Operating Rules
1. $0 spend means $0: no boosts, no trials that convert to paid, no paid app installs.
2. Never invent performance claims — report clicks, sessions, and sales from real sources only.
3. The Merchant Center feed must be re-validated after any catalog change (catalog is frozen — flag, don't edit).
