# Caption Formula

## Structure
1. **Hook line** — episode title or POV opener, all caps for saga parts: `PAROLE SAGA — PART 8: THE SHAKEDOWN`
2. **Dark comedic body** — 2–4 short lines, "or else" tagline energy. Example: `He never bought the chew toy. Now the crew knows where he sleeps.`
3. **Product line** — name the product as the plot device + individual UTM URL: `?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=daily_five`
4. **Verdict-bait CTA** (always): `⚖️ DROP THE VERDICT: GUILTY or GOOD BOY? 👇`
5. **Sales CTA** (only on/before 2026-10-08): `🎟️ 20% OFF with code GOODBOY20 — ends Oct 8!`
6. **End tagline** (always): `Who's A Good Boy Pet Accessories & Toys — before your pet goes bad.`
7. **Hashtags** — quirky mix: `#whosagoodboy #parolesaga #guiltyorgoodboy #petsoftiktok #darkcomedy` + 2–3 product/pet tags.

## Voice
Dark, playful menace. Short sentences. Never explain the joke. Never invent discount codes or deadlines — GOODBOY20 ends Oct 8, 2026.

## Viral one-off openers (rotate)
- `POV: It's 3 AM and you never bought the [product]...`
- `MUGSHOT ➡️ MOST WANTED ✨`
- `The crew left this on your doorstep.`
- `He was a good boy. Was.`
