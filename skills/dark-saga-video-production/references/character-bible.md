# Character Bible (verbatim — paste into generation prompts)

## Dread Lock the Dog
Rugged brown doodle with long dark dreadlocks, red-yellow-green rasta knit beanie, thick gold chain, expressive human-like face, chewed rope toy in mouth. Streetwise, cool-uncle energy. Leader of the night crew.

## Sir Whiskers the Cat
Sleek tuxedo cat with tiny designer bowtie, sunglasses, slicked-back fur, human-like judging posture. Bougie CEO energy. The crew's strategist and enforcer.

## Rules
- Photoreal only — realistic fur, eyes, fabric, light, physics, camera/lens language. NEVER cartoon.
- Keep both characters visually consistent across episodes: same beanie, same chain, same bowtie, same sunglasses.
- Human-like expressions and posture, but they remain unmistakably a dog and a cat.
- No on-screen text in generated video — captions and taglines live in the post copy and voiceover only.
