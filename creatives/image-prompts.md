# Image Prompts — Midjourney / Flux

Photorealistic only — real fur, real fabric, real light. No cartoon, no illustration, no Pixar.
Recommended: `--ar 4:5` for Meta feed, `--ar 9:16` for TikTok/Reels. Append `--style raw` (Midjourney) for maximum realism.

## Prompt 1 — "The Survivor" (durable dog toy)

Pairs with: Video Script 1 — "The Toy Graveyard"

```
Photorealistic action photo of a happy golden retriever mid-chew on a thick dark
rubber training bite stick toy, standing in a sunny suburban backyard, shallow
depth of field, toy gripped firmly in jaws showing its textured durable surface,
warm late-afternoon sunlight, green grass softly blurred behind, water droplets
on fur, ultra-detailed fur texture, shot on 85mm f/1.8 --ar 4:5 --style raw
Negative: cartoon, illustration, 3d render, pixar, blurry toy, deformed teeth
```

## Usage notes

- Generate at the target aspect ratio — never crop 1:1 into 9:16.
- Keep product colors true to the store listings; don't recolor toys/harnesses.
- Burn the store logo bug into the lower third only on final ad exports, never in the base photo.
- Pair each photo with its matching video script for hook continuity.
